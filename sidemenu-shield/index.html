What changed

  1. Rows are grouped into four cards under headings, on a soft gray canvas.
  2. The Inspect and Payments accordions are gone; their children sit directly in a group.
  3. The name row is a profile card with an initials avatar and role.
  4. Icons are filled and sit in tinted circles.
  5. Labels are near-black. Blue is kept for the active item, shown as a tinted pill.
  6. Every queue shows a count pill; orange marks the ones needing attention.
  7. The drawer has rounded corners and a shadow, and the inbox behind it is blurred.
  8. Sign out is a quiet button at the bottom.

Counts, the role label and the message previews are placeholders. Colors are the prototype's existing navy, blue and orange tokens.
