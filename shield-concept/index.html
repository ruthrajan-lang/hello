Introducing WiFi Motion
Away or asleep, stay aware of what's happening at home

Your WiFi can detect movement in your home without installing anything new.

What it is, and what it isn't
Sends you an alert when it senses movement
Isn't monitored and won't call for help
No cameras.
From pets to kids, you decide what's worth a heads up

Check that the kids got home from school or that the dog walker stopped by on time. You can adjust motion sensitivity.

Notifications that work for you

Use Home Watch for fewer notifications while you're moving around, Away Watch for real-time alerts when you're out, and Dark Watch overnight.

Turn it on and off anytime

WiFi Motion is an included feature that you can turn off and on at any time.

Important tips
Tips to make WiFi Motion work best for your home

Detection areas are between your Xfinity Gateway and other suitable WiFi devices in your home.

Select devices in areas where you want to detect movement, like entryways and common rooms. They should be distanced from the WiFi Gateway, stationary, and always on.
