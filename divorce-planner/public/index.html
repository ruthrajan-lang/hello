Colorado is an equitable distribution state: marital property is divided fairly, not necessarily 50/50. Property owned before the marriage, gifts and inheritances are generally separate, but their increase in value during the marriage is marital. Income and maintenance estimate

Colorado's advisory guideline (C.R.S. 14-10-114) for combined income up to $240,000/yr: maintenance = 40% of combined monthly adjusted gross income minus the lower earner's income. The term is 31% of the marriage length at 3 years, rising to 50% at 20 years; under 3 years is discretionary, over 20 may be indefinite. The court can deviate. Child support uses a separate income-shares formula: use the official Colorado child support calculator and record the result below.
